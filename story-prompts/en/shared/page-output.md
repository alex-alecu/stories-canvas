## Required Page Fields And Text Limits

- Each page must include pageNumber, text, imagePrompt, and characters. Do not omit a field.
- Keep each page's text at or below {{max_chars}} characters, including spaces and punctuation, and {{max_sentences}} sentences. Aim below the limit to leave room for dialogue.
- These limits also apply to faithful retellings. Keep the cause, action, and result; shorten descriptions and repeated travel first.
- The characters field must be a nonempty array of exact names from the story's character definitions. Include every named character visible in imagePrompt.
- Every declared character must appear on at least one page. Do not mention an absent character by name inside imagePrompt.
- Check these fields and limits before submitting the complete script. Do not substitute word counts for character counts.
