# Story Generator Template

Use this as a reusable prompt for story ideation, scenario drafting, or structured story generation.

```text
Write an original {{language}} story for children age {{age}}.

Requirements:
- Center the story on one clear problem, quest, or test.
- Use a small, lovable protagonist with a child-readable motivation.
- Keep the cast small, the plot linear, and the scenes easy to picture.
- Open on a memorable image and make the emotional arc easy to follow.
- Use repetition or a rule-of-three pattern with at least one setback.
- Let the protagonist solve the problem actively through kindness, courage, honesty, teamwork, or cleverness.
- Include one or two magical or wondrous elements, but keep the rules simple.
- Keep danger gentle and non-graphic.
- Use concrete language, visible action, and a read-aloud rhythm.
- End with comfort, fairness, belonging, celebration, or delight.
- Avoid cruelty overload, moral muddle, passive heroes, cluttered subplots, and bleak endings.

Story premise:
{{user_prompt}}

Optional constraints:
- Tone: {{tone}}
- Setting: {{setting}}
- Protagonist: {{protagonist}}
- Emotional lesson: {{lesson}}
- Length / page count: {{length}}
- Output format: {{output_contract}}
```
